HOW TO PLAY

🎮
Controls

WASD to move, aim with the mouse, click to shoot. SPACE or right-click for your SUPER.Left thumb moves. Right thumb: drag to aim, let go to shoot (tap = auto-aim). Yellow button = SUPER.

💎
Number Grab

The RULE at the top (like PRIMES) changes every 35 s. Only grab gems that fit it. Wrong gems zap you and cost a gem!

❓
Brain Cubes

Smash purple ? crates to pocket Brain Cubes. Open them with 🧠 (Q) whenever you like: solve the problem for +10% health and damage.

🧠
The 🧠 button

One button for math power: it smashes a nearby Math Shield, opens a Brain Cube, or charges your SUPER. Missed? You get one more try.

💫
Comebacks

Knocked out? Solve a problem to respawn instantly. In Royale you get one Second Chance.

🗺️
Adventure

Play stages one by one, earn up to 3 ⭐ each, and beat the bosses. Progress is saved to the cloud.

✏️
Scratch pad

Hard problem? Open the scratch pad and work it out. The game is paused while you think.
